What Surfer does

Three jobs in Chrome: dwell, text, reading mode.

01

Fewer forced clicks

Hold still over a link or button and Surfer completes the activation. Fewer missed targets when your hand won't cooperate that hour. Dwell clicking works best on desktop.

02

Type you can actually read

Increase size, widen line spacing, pick a clearer font, and boost contrast on the live page, not in a separate reader you have to leave for.

03

Reading mode when the layout fights you

Strip chrome and sidebars to one column. Focus mode can step paragraph by paragraph, including with dwell if your hands are off the mouse.

04

Settings that stay local

Preferences live in Chrome storage on your device. Banking, shopping, email, news: same tools, your choices, no Axol account required.

How to rest your eyes online

By a tired reader · 4 min read

Long pages stack ads, autoplay video, and newsletter prompts on every edge. Your eyes leave the sentence, find a blinking promo, and lose the place they were holding.

Reading mode pulls the article into one calm column. Type size, line height, and background follow controls you choose. Sidebars and site chrome stay behind the overlay.

Focus mode can highlight a single paragraph and advance when you are ready, or on dwell if pressing is hard. Fatigue compensation can grow size and spacing as you read farther down a long piece.

Hard words can show a simpler alternative on hover. Everything runs on your device. The page content never leaves the browser for this feature.

How it compares

Tuned for the browser, not the OS.

OS dwell tools sit in system settings with one timing for the whole computer. Surfer runs in Chrome, where you browse, and can treat a YouTube thumbnail differently from a player button or a text link. Dwell clicking works best on desktop.

OS-level dwell tools

  • Buried in system accessibility settings
  • One dwell time for the whole computer
  • No awareness of video players or in-page UI
  • No font, size, or spacing controls for the page itself

Accessibility Surfer

  • Chrome extension, available on every site you open
  • Separate dwell times for YouTube videos, YouTube buttons, and other sites
  • Per-thumbnail countdown ring and a clear on-page status
  • Built-in text controls: font, size, line height, letter and word spacing
